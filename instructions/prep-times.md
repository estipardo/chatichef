# Real times: how to work them out

Upload it as a project document. The instructions refer to it. Spanish version: `prep-times.es.md`.

## The problem

Recipe times are usually **stove time** (sofrito + cooking + resting), with peeling and chopping tucked "inside" the first steps as if they took no time. In a recipe with four vegetables that's 15–25 minutes nobody counted. And someone at a beginner level cuts more slowly than an internet recipe, which also tends to assume the vegetables are already prepared. Typical result: "55-minute" lentils that take 1 h 30.

## Required convention

Three numbers, never one:

1. **Knife** — peeling, washing, topping and tailing, chopping, slicing, drying meat or fish, draining jars, getting the pans out. It's standing-up work, and **it's the number that matters most**.
2. **Heat** — cooking, oven, air fryer, microwave and resting, marking which part is waiting.
3. **Realistic total** = the sum, minus only what really overlaps (the microwave or air fryer working while you cut something else). **Say what overlaps with what.**

And say what is **not** included: washing up, and the time spent looking for the jar or the tin.

## Reference table (beginner pace)

Adjust it to your real pace after a couple of weeks.

| Task | Time |
|---|---|
| Peel and cut 1 potato into slices or cubes | 2–3 min (300–350 g ≈ 6–8 min) |
| Broken potato chunks (knife tip and twist) | 7 min for 350 g |
| Peel and finely chop 1 whole carrot | 3–4 min |
| Cut 1 fresh pepper into strips or cubes | 2–3 min |
| Slice 4–5 garlic cloves | 3 min |
| Top and tail 300 g fresh green beans | 6–8 min — always underestimated (frozen: 0) |
| Wash and drain 300 g spinach | 3 min |
| Wipe and slice 250 g mushrooms | 5 min |
| Cut 400–440 g of meat or chicken into 2–3 cm cubes | 6–8 min |
| Pat dry and salt meat, fish, prawns or squid | 3–5 min |
| Grate 1 tomato / slice tomatoes | 2–3 min |
| Drain and rinse a jar of pulses / drain cans of tuna | 1–2 min |
| Beat eggs and set up a coating (flour + egg in two plates) | 4–5 min |
| Get pans, board and knife out, and turn the oven on | 2–3 min |

## Estimating rules

- Each different **fresh** vegetable to peel or chop = **at least 3 minutes**.
- A stew with a sofrito of three fresh vegetables + potato **never goes under 20 minutes of knife**.
- Preheating the oven (10–12 min) overlaps with the assembly, but **it has to be mentioned**.
- The microwave and the air fryer only overlap with cutting if the recipe says so, in that order.
- With cutting counted, almost no recipe goes under 20 minutes total: speed is measured by the knife, not the clock.

## Frozen vegetables: when they change the result

- **Frozen pepper**: perfect in stews, sofrito, air-fryer trays and pizza (on pizza, 1 min in the microwave and pat dry). **Bad** for fried peppers and high-heat stir-fries: it releases water and stews instead of frying.
- **Frozen green beans**: 7 minutes in the microwave with 3 tablespoons of water, and **drain them very well** if they go into a cream sauce.
- **Grated carrot**: fine for sofritos and stews; **not for julienne** in stir-fries, it falls apart.

## How to cut down knife time (the only thing that really saves time)

- Frozen vegetables, already prepared.
- Ask the butcher to cube the meat: 6–8 min less per recipe.
- Fewer vegetables per dish: two instead of four.
- Whole potato in the microwave and then cut, instead of peeling and cutting it raw.
- Bagged salad or sliced tomato when the cutting is already heavy.
- Cutting heat time **saves nothing**: it's time you can spend sitting down.
